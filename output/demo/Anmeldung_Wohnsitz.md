# Anmeldung Wohnsitz

The "Anmeldung Wohnsitz" service provides an online form for registering a move to Ausserberg. This service enables users to update their residence information efficiently and is accessible through the official Ausserberg website.

## Required Information

To complete the registration, the following details must be provided:

- **Salutation**: Herr or Frau
- **First Name**: Mandatory field
- **Last Name**: Mandatory field
- **Address of Previous Residence**: Mandatory field
- **New Address in Ausserberg**: Mandatory field
- **Telephone Number**
- **Email Address**
- **Your Message**: Mandatory field

Additionally, you must confirm that you have read and accepted the [Privacy Policy](https://www.ausserberg.ch/zusaetzliches/datenschutz). This is a mandatory requirement for submitting the form.

For more information and access to the form, visit the [Anmeldung Wohnsitz Page](https://www.ausserberg.ch/gemeinschaft/verwaltung/verwaltung/online-schalter/anmeldung-wohnsitz).
